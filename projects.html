Electrical & Programming Lead

NASA Human Exploration Rover Challenge (HERC) · University of Central Missouri

I led the Electrical and Programming subteams to a first-place national finish in the 2026 NASA HERC College/University Division.

I designed a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W embedded instrumentation system for real-time environmental sensing and telemetry. I integrated soil-moisture, pH, and CO₂ sensors using ADC, I²C, and UART, and developed MicroPython firmware for synchronized sensor polling.

I implemented BLE telemetry and redundant UART communication, then conducted integration and performance validation, including a four-day runtime test.

  • MicroPython
  • Embedded sensing
  • BLE telemetry
