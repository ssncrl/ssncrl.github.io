Sep. 2025–Apr. 2026

Embedded Instrumentation System

NASA Human Exploration Rover Challenge (HERC) · University of Central Missouri. Led the Electrical and Programming subteams to a first-place national finish in the 2026 College/University Division.

Designed a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W system for real-time environmental sensing and telemetry. Integrated soil-moisture, pH, and CO₂ sensors over ADC, I²C, and UART; developed MicroPython firmware for synchronized polling; and implemented BLE telemetry with redundant UART communication. Validated integration and performance, including a four-day runtime test.

  • Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W
  • MicroPython
  • Embedded sensing
  • BLE telemetry
