Hi, I'm Susan-Carol.

I'm a software engineering student at the University of Central Missouri, with a focus on robotics and automation. I'm interested in building adaptive robotic systems that improve accessibility, independence, and everyday life.

I enjoy working where software meets hardware, from embedded systems and mechanical design to interfaces that help people interact with technology. Much of my current work explores how robots can understand human needs and provide useful, safe assistance.

What I'm curious about

Research interests

Assistive Technology and Accessible Design. I'm interested in developing human-centered technologies that reduce barriers in daily living and help people with disabilities live more independently.

Assistive Robotics and Human-Robot Interaction. I want to develop intelligent robots that respond to human intent and provide safe, adaptive physical assistance through compliant control, soft robotics, and force sensing.

Sensory Augmentation and Spatial Interaction. I'm interested in using spatial computing, natural-language processing, and computer vision to augment human perception and make the physical world more accessible, from helping visually impaired people navigate their surroundings to enabling more intuitive interactions with assistive technologies.
