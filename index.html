01 / About

Engineering with purpose.

I'm interested in building intelligent robotic systems that understand, interact with, assist, and augment people. I enjoy working across the boundary between software and physical hardware, from sensing and control to system integration and experimental testing.

My experience includes assistive robotics research, engineering team leadership, and mixed-reality interfaces. I'm continuing to develop my skills in robotics, embedded programming, and experimental design.

I'm particularly interested in assistive technology, safe physical human-robot interaction, and robotic systems designed around people's needs.
