Selected research

Assistive robotics · Undergraduate Research · Experimental Design

Missing Middle: Assistive Robotic Bathing Tool for Mobility-Limited Adults

Research and development of a soft-contact robotic bathing end-effector intended to support mobility-limited adults.

My contributions include end-effector design, integrating compliant materials with a rigid structure, force-sensing calibration, robotic control integration, and safety-oriented testing.

My work explores how compliant mechanical design and force-aware control can contribute to assistive physical interaction.

Under the McNair Scholars Program, I developed a research question, conducted scholarly writing, planned experiments, and reviewed relevant literature to support my research in assistive robotics.

Mixed reality

Semantic Querying for Mixed-Reality Interfaces

A comparative study of semantic querying and traditional text search using a HoloLens-based interface. The research examines information retrieval and the role of language-based interaction in mixed reality.

The project includes experimental planning and evaluation of different search approaches.
